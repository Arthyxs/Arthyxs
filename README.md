### About me

IT technician with **5 years of programming** and hands-on IT support experience. At a logistics company I designed and built the internal systems the team relies on every day: integration APIs on top of legacy systems, a sales performance dashboard, daily bank reconciliation, an IT asset inventory and an employee portal.

I focus on **fast, well-scoped delivery**: requirements confirmed in writing, regular updates, documented code and clean handover.

- **Automation & scripts**: Python, Node.js and PowerShell for repetitive tasks, scraping and data processing
- **Spreadsheets**: advanced Excel and Google Sheets, VBA macros, Apps Script, automated reports and dashboards
- **APIs & integrations**: connecting systems, normalizing data, serverless backends on Cloudflare Workers
- **Web apps**: React + TypeScript dashboards and internal tools
- **IT support**: networks, tooling and troubleshooting
